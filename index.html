THE DUSTY KILN

Made by hand, meant for everyday life.

What started as a hobby quickly became a love for making useful, beautiful things by hand. I fell in love with the process of turning a simple lump of clay into something meant to be used, shared, and enjoyed every day.

Today, I make small-batch stoneware pottery in Central Michigan, inspired by simple forms, natural textures, and the little rituals of everyday life. From your morning coffee to gathering around the table, I hope each piece brings a little more intention to the moments you already enjoy.
