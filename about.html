About the Artist

I'm a hobby potter based in Central Michigan. Much of my work is influenced by the things I enjoy outside the studio: time outdoors, country living, and the details of the places and activities that make up everyday life.

Those interests find their way into my pottery in different forms, from relatively simple functional pieces to more playful, themed collections. I like having room to experiment rather than committing to one particular style.

The Dusty Kiln is a place to share that work as it develops. Some ideas stick around, others turn into something unexpected, and there's always another project waiting to be tried.
